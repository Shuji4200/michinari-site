      How We Work

      To Grow Sales, We First Sort Out the Challenges and the Order of Work

      We confirm which stage you are in and choose only what fits it. We check in the same order when improving marketing for an existing business.

      1. 01Confirm the customer’s problem

        Check what customers truly struggle with and why they buy

        CPF
      2. 02Design the solution

        Decide how to sell and who to reach

        PSF
      3. 03Shape the product

        Build LPs, LINE and more on a small scale

        SPF
      4. 04Test real response

        Compare inquiries and deals with the plan using small ad tests

        PMF
      5. 05Scale what worked

        Shift budget to methods that pay off

        GTM
      6. 06Build a team that keeps improving

        Move operations in-house and keep improving

        Growth

      The six stages are based on the “Fit Journey” toward PMF (when your product is chosen by the market). Created by MICHINARI based on Sairu, “PMF FAQ” (September 28, 2026)

      Why PMF

      Before Increasing Ad Spend, We Check the Path to Sales

      Until you pass the summit (PMF), it takes effort, like pushing a rock uphill. Past it, investment turns into results more easily.

      Needs improvementBefore PMF

      • Spending budget doesn’t increase inquiries
      • Inquiries don’t become meetings
      • Meetings rarely close
      • Costs don’t pay off, so budget can’t continue

      Target stateAfter PMF

      • Inquiries turn into meetings
      • Meetings turn into deals
      • Referrals and repeat customers grow
      • Costs pay off, so you can decide on more investment
